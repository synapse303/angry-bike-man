# Angry Bike Man 🚲💢

**▶ Play now:** https://synapse303.github.io/angry-bike-man/ (works best on a phone held sideways)

A 3D overtaking game in the style of 80s arcade racers. Get past the furious Lycra-clad cyclist without knocking him off, while you dodge the oncoming traffic.
He mirrors your every move, shouts (censored) abuse at you, and from level 4 starts throwing his water bottles, bananas and D-locks at you.

- 8-bit rock soundtrack, made entirely with Web Audio
- Touch controls (◀ ▶ / GAS / BRAKE / HONK) or keyboard (arrows or WASD, Space to honk)
- Android app: [download AngryBikeMan.apk](https://github.com/synapse303/angry-bike-man/raw/main/AngryBikeMan.apk)

Everything is one self-contained `index.html`. The `android/` folder holds the WebView wrapper used to build the APK.
